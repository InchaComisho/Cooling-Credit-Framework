## Overview

The solution to global warming is not simply to cool the planet artificially with new machines.

The deeper objective is to **restore the weakened natural phenomena of the Earth and restart the natural cooling functions that the planet originally possessed**.

Earth originally had multiple functions that dissipated heat, circulated water, and stabilized life-supporting systems.

```text
Water circulation
Evaporation
Evapotranspiration
Cloud formation
Rainfall
Soil water retention
Forest-based cooling
Carbon fixation through microbes and humus
Ocean circulation
Carbon fixation through phytoplankton
```

As these functions weaken, land surfaces dry out, cities accumulate heat, forests lose transpiration capacity, oceans stratify, soil microbial networks collapse, and the Earth’s natural cooling capacity declines.

Cooling Credits assign economic value to actions that restore these natural cooling functions and make society implement them.

---

## Core Definition

> **Cooling Credits are a proposed system that assigns economic value to actions that restore and restart the Earth’s original natural cooling functions, redirecting human desire, profit, investment, and business toward natural regeneration.**

In shorter terms:

> **A system that gives value to actions that reduce heat.**

But the deeper purpose is not merely local cooling.

> **The purpose is to help Earth regain its own capacity to cool itself.**

## Why It Must Be Built into Profit Incentives

The broad direction of the solution to global warming is already visible.

It is not only emissions reduction, but also the restoration of natural cooling functions:

```text
Return water to soils
Return evapotranspiration to forests
Return water cycles to cities
Return vertical circulation and oxygen to the ocean
Return carbon fixation to microbes, humus, and vegetation
Return cloud and rainfall cycles
```

However, humanity does not move sufficiently simply because something is morally correct.

Goodwill alone is rarely enough to change industrial systems, urban systems, financial systems, and consumption systems.

Therefore, Cooling Credits are designed to redirect human desire, profit, investment, business, and institutional incentives away from nature-destroying systems and toward nature-regenerating systems.

> **Cooling Credits are not a moral argument for protecting nature. They are a system for replacing the engine of natural destruction with an engine of natural regeneration.**

## Difference from Carbon Credits

| Aspect | Carbon Credit | Cooling Credit |
|---|---|---|
| Main focus | CO₂ reduction and offsetting | Real-world heat-load reduction and natural cooling-function recovery |
| Core metric | Carbon accounting | Temperature, WBGT, water cycle, soil moisture, evapotranspiration, ocean circulation |
| Main purpose | Manage emissions | Restore Earth’s cooling capacity |
| Weakness | May become book-based offsetting | Requires MRV and physical verification |
| Role | Input-side measure | Output-side measure and natural-function restart |

Carbon Credits remain important for emissions reduction and carbon fixation.

However, Earth does not cool down through accounting alone.

Earth cools when water circulates, forests transpire, soils retain moisture, clouds form, rain falls, oceans circulate, and microbes and plants fix carbon.

Cooling action

This is not a proposal to dominate nature artificially.

---

## Caution

This concept does not claim that more clouds always produce cooling.

Water vapor and clouds can either cool or warm depending on altitude, season, region, time of day, and cloud type.

Therefore, Cooling Credits should not evaluate simple sunlight blocking or water-vapor increase alone. They should evaluate integrated restoration of soil, forests, water cycles, ocean circulation, and ecosystems.
