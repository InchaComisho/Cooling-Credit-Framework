# Provisional Cooling Credit Unit Definition

[← Back to Cooling Credit Framework README](../README.md)

---

## Overview

This document defines an initial evaluation unit for using Cooling Credits in pilot projects, score estimation, and institutional design.

The unit defined here is not a formal financial product, legal credit, or international certification unit.

It is a **Provisional Cooling Credit Unit** for early-stage testing, comparison, and framework development.

---

## Basic Definition

> **One Provisional Cooling Credit Unit is an initial evaluation unit for actions that contribute to measurable or estimable heat-load reduction, cooling demand reduction, water-cycle recovery, soil water retention, vegetation recovery, or natural cooling-function restoration within a defined period and area.**

In simpler terms:

```text
1 Provisional Cooling Credit Unit
= an early evaluation unit for measurable cooling contribution
```

---

## Why a Provisional Unit Is Needed

Cooling Credits cannot be evaluated in exactly the same way as a simple carbon-ton unit.

They involve multiple categories of impact:

```text
air temperature reduction
WBGT reduction
surface temperature reduction
cooling electricity demand reduction
soil moisture recovery
evaporation and evapotranspiration recovery
vegetation recovery
rainwater and reclaimed-water use
organic-matter circulation
ocean circulation support
maintenance continuity
```

Therefore, at the early stage, Cooling Credits should be treated as a provisional integrated evaluation unit rather than a single absolute universal unit.

---

## Evaluation Targets

A Provisional Cooling Credit Unit may evaluate the following types of effects.

### 1. Temperature Indicators

```text
air temperature reduction
surface temperature reduction
WBGT reduction
nighttime heat-storage reduction
urban surface-temperature reduction
```

### 2. Energy Indicators

```text
cooling electricity demand reduction
peak electricity demand reduction
public-facility cooling load reduction
transport-facility cooling demand reduction
```

### 3. Water-Cycle Indicators

```text
rainwater storage
reclaimed-water use
soil infiltration
soil moisture recovery
evaporation and evapotranspiration recovery
longer water-retention time
```

### 4. Ecosystem and Soil Indicators

```text
humus production
organic-matter circulation
soil organic matter
microbial recovery
vegetation recovery
canopy cover
forest cooling function
```

### 5. Ocean and Coastal Indicators

```text
water temperature change
dissolved oxygen improvement
seawater circulation support
seaweed-bed restoration
biomass recovery
fishery recovery
```

## Provisional Scoring Formula

For early pilots, an integrated scoring approach may be used:

```text
Provisional Cooling Credit Unit
= Temperature Score
+ WBGT Score
+ Surface Cooling Score
+ Cooling Demand Reduction Score
+ Soil Water Score
+ Vegetation Score
+ Water Cycle Score
+ Organic Matter Score
+ Maintenance Score
- Risk Adjustment
```

This is not an international standard.

It is a provisional formula for comparison, improvement, and pilot evaluation.

---

## Period and Area

A provisional unit must always specify the evaluation period and area.

Examples:

```text
30 days / schoolyard
90 days / station plaza
one year / urban park
one year / watershed forest
90 days / farm plot
one year / coastal fishery area
```

Credits without a defined period and area should be avoided.

## Candidate Metrics

Future institutional design may combine indicators such as:

```text
°C · m² · hour
WBGT reduction hour
kWh cooling demand avoided
soil moisture recovery index
evapotranspiration recovery index
vegetation recovery index
water retention index
organic matter circulation index
marine cooling support index
```

At the early stage, these should be treated as components of the Cooling Score, not direct financial units.

---

## Principles

A Provisional Cooling Credit Unit should follow these principles:

```text
measurable impact
clear period
clear area
baseline comparison
maintenance records
sustainable water source
side-effect review
not an emissions excuse
```

---

## Cases That Should Not Generate Units

A project should not generate provisional units if:

```text
there are no measurements
there is no before-and-after comparison
the period is unclear
the area is unclear
maintenance is absent
water-source burden is excessive
local environmental side effects are not checked
it is used only for advertising
it is used as an excuse for emissions
```

---

## Use During the Pilot Stage

At the early stage, provisional units can be used for:

```text
pilot comparison
business-model evaluation
municipal decision-making support
investment reference
research model testing
Cooling Score improvement
institutional design prototypes
```

Formal financial products, trading markets, and legal credit systems would require additional MRV, third-party verification, standardization, regional correction, and risk management.
