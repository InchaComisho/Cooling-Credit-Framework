## Basic Policy

This concept is openly published for the public interest and for the restoration of the Earth system.

However, public disclosure does not mean that the contribution of the original proposer may be ignored.

If governments, municipalities, companies, research institutions, investors, or organizations use this concept for implementation, commercialization, institutional design, research, or investment decisions, they are requested to provide clear credit to the original proposer, **Master / inchacomusho / InchaComisho**, and to consider appropriate forms of support, collaboration, sponsorship, joint research, consulting, or implementation partnership.

## Why Return of Value Matters

The Cooling Credit Framework and its related concepts in Natural Complementary Science, ocean circulation recovery, urban cooling, desert regeneration, forest regeneration, organic-resource circulation, transportation and public-facility cooling, and business-model design have been observed, conceptualized, structured, and publicly released by an independent individual over time.

They are published for public benefit. Yet if they are used for implementation, commercialization, or institutional design, it is not desirable for the original intellectual contribution to be consumed without recognition or return.

The concept is open.  
But if it is used for implementation, commercialization, or institutional design, value should return to the original proposer.

Return of value does not necessarily mean money alone.

- sponsorship,
- donations or support,
- paid consultation,
