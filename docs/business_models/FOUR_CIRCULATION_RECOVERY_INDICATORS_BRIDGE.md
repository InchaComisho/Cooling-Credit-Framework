## Model Connections

### 1. EEZ Fishery Recovery Cooling Credit Business Model

The primary target is ocean circulation. The model supports ocean carbon fixation, oxygen supply, and food circulation through oxygen supply, vertical circulation, surface heat suppression, and phytoplankton foundation recovery.

- Main indicators: sea-surface temperature, dissolved oxygen, chlorophyll, vertical mixing, fishery recovery indicators
- Connected circulations: ocean circulation, food circulation, water phase-transition circulation

### 2. Tourism Resource Recovery Cooling Credit Model

Tourism resource recovery is connected to natural cooling, water-cycle recovery, landscape restoration, and ecosystem regeneration across beaches, lakes, forests, and urban waterfronts.

- Main indicators: surface temperature, water quality, ecosystem recovery, landscape recovery, tourism revenue
- Connected circulations: water phase-transition circulation, atmospheric circulation, ocean circulation

### 3. Desert Circular Pyramid City Business Model

This model transforms drylands into cooling, residential, and food-production zones through water, humus, greening, mist, shade, and renewable energy.

- Main indicators: land-surface temperature, soil moisture, greening area, evapotranspiration, cooling load reduction
- Connected circulations: water phase-transition circulation, atmospheric circulation, organic matter circulation

### 4. Urban Green Infrastructure Cooling Credit Model

This model transforms urban heat-storing structures into cooling structures through water cycles, greening, water-retentive pavements, mist, and humus circulation.

- Main indicators: WBGT, urban temperature, surface temperature, rainwater use, shade ratio, evapotranspiration
- Connected circulations: water phase-transition circulation, atmospheric circulation, organic matter circulation

### 5. Monoculture Mountain Forest to Native-Fruit Mixed Forest Model

This model converts monoculture or abandoned forests into multilayered natural forests, fruit forests, watershed forests, wild edible forests, mushroom forests, and nectar forests.

- Main indicators: forest soil carbon, spring flow, evapotranspiration, humus, biodiversity
- Connected circulations: organic matter circulation, water phase-transition circulation, atmospheric circulation

### 6. Center Mist Ultrasonic Cooling Fan Model

This low-entry local cooling model evaluates measurable cooling, WBGT improvement, and air-conditioning load reduction.

- Main indicators: local air temperature, WBGT, humidity, cooling electricity reduction, mist cooling volume
- Connected circulations: water phase-transition circulation, atmospheric circulation

### 7. Food Loss and Organic Waste to Humus Model

This model avoids incineration and returns organic matter to soil, restoring organic matter circulation, soil water retention, microorganisms, evapotranspiration, and carbon fixation.

- Main indicators: food residue collection, avoided incineration, humus, soil organic carbon, soil moisture
- Connected circulations: food and organic matter circulation, water phase-transition circulation, atmospheric circulation

### 8. Organic Matter Soil Recovery and Desert Greening Model

This integrated model connects humification, soil recovery, dryland greening, and desert-edge regeneration.

- Main indicators: humus input, soil moisture, vegetation recovery, evapotranspiration, land-surface temperature, carbon fixation
- Connected circulations: organic matter circulation, water phase-transition circulation, atmospheric circulation
